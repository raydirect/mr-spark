What changed this issue

The harbour office re-surveyed the bar in September, which moved the afternoon highs down by about 0.2 m on spring tides. Charts from before issue 40 are wrong by that much.

Corrections and arguments both go to tides@northbay.example. We publish a correction note when somebody is right, and we name them.
