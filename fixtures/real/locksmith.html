Locked out, or locking up

My father opened this van in 1994. It is still one van and one locksmith, and we still quote before we touch anything.

Out of hours: if the phone goes to voicemail after 9pm, we are on a job. Leave the street and we call back, usually inside twenty minutes.

Work and prices

Job Typical
Car or door unlock, no damage £68

Call-out inside the four wards is in those figures. Twenty-five miles out it is not, and I will tell you what it is before I leave the yard.

Where we go

Bridgeway, Mill Row, the two estates past the water, and anything the lane reaches without a permit. We do not do alarm systems or cars with smart keys — a dealer is honest about those and we would rather send you to one.
