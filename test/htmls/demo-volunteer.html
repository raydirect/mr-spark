Join the crew

Tell us what moves you and when you are free. We will match you and a coordinator will be in touch.

How much time do I need?
As little as one afternoon. Every role lists its hours up front, from one three-hour shift to a weekly slot over a school year.
Do I need special skills?
No. Most roles train you on the day. If you have a trade, teaching or digital skills, we list projects that use them.
Can I volunteer from home?
Yes. Choose Remote on the board for tutoring calls, phone check-ins, translation and design projects.
