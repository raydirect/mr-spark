Our Beekeepers

The people behind
every jar.

We visit every beekeeper we work with. These are not anonymous bulk suppliers. They are third-generation apiarists, former farmers who converted their land, and passionate naturalists who built apiaries in rainforest country specifically to produce one thing well.

Tarkine Wilderness, Tasmania

Margaret Keown

Leatherwood Honey

Margaret's 60 hives are placed at the edge of temperate rainforest where the leatherwood tree flowers for a narrow six-week window each summer. The resulting honey is deeply floral with a spiced finish unlike anything else Australia produces.

"Leatherwood doesn't grow anywhere else in the world at this scale. I'm very aware of what we're custodians of."
Central Tablelands, NSW

Darren Liu

Yellow Box Honey

Darren manages apiaries across 800 hectares of Yellow Box woodland, one of the most threatened ecosystems in Australia. His honey is considered by many to be the finest in the country for raw eating, mellow and crystalline-bright with a long finish.

"Yellow Box only flowers every two or three years. You learn to be patient. The honey makes it worth it."
Sunshine Coast Hinterland, QLD

Ross Brindley

Macadamia Blossom Honey

Ross's apiaries sit inside a working macadamia orchard in the ranges behind Noosa. The bees pollinate the trees while producing a honey with a soft buttery sweetness and a warm, almost caramel finish, completely unlike commercially available macadamia honey.

"Most macadamia honey on the shelf is a blend. Mine is pure blossom from these orchards. That's the difference."

What's the difference between raw and filtered honey?

Raw honey is extracted from the comb at room temperature and bottled with minimal filtration, leaving natural enzymes, pollen, propolis and antioxidants intact. Filtered honey is heated and passed through fine filters to remove all particles, which extends shelf life and keeps the product clear, but removes much of what makes honey nutritionally distinctive. Most supermarket honey is filtered. None of ours is.

How long does raw honey last?

Properly stored, raw honey has an indefinite shelf life. Keep the lid tight and store away from direct sunlight or heat. Many of our varietals will granulate over time as they cool, this is entirely natural and a sign the honey is genuine. To return granulated honey to a pourable state, place the jar in warm water for 20 minutes. Do not microwave.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within Australia only. International shipment of raw honey faces strict biosecurity restrictions in most countries, and we've decided not to compromise our product with the heat treatment required to clear customs. We're watching developments in this area carefully and hope to ship to New Zealand and parts of Europe in 2027.

Can I choose which varietal I receive?

The whole point of the subscription is discovery, so we curate each month's selection. You will always know what's coming in your confirmation email 10 days before dispatch, and you can skip a month or swap if a particular varietal doesn't suit you. Apiary Box subscribers can also maintain a preference profile noting any flavours they'd like to see more or less of.

Is CanvasHive honey certified organic?

Several of our producers hold certified organic status, and we note this clearly in each beekeeper's profile and on the jar label. Organic certification for honey is rigorous in Australia and requires that hives be placed beyond flight distance of any conventional farming operation. Where producers are not certified, we visit their apiaries personally and assess the surrounding land use before listing their honey.

How do I cancel or pause my subscription?

Log into your account and you'll find cancel and pause options under Subscription Settings. Cancellations made before the 20th of the month take effect before the next billing cycle. There are no fees, no phone calls required, and no guilt trips. We'd rather you cancel and come back when the timing's right than stay subscribed when it's not working for you.
